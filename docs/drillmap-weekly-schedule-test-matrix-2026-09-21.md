## Notes for review

- The Saturday practice test remains scheduled even when it exceeds stated weekend capacity. Scenario 9 makes the exception easiest to see.
- Sunday review always equals the configured weekend capacity.
- The 1.5x and 2x account categories yield 187.5- and 225-minute Saturday tests, respectively.
- Regular RC passage time stays unchanged by accommodations. LR drills and full sections grow with the accommodation setting.
