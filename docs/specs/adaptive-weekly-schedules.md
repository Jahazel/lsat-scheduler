# Adaptive weekly schedules, policy v3

> Status: implemented in the current checkout. Saved `adaptive-weekly-schedule-v1` and `adaptive-weekly-schedule-v2` schedules retain their original plan and display. The tutor answers behind v3 are in [spec #191](https://github.com/Jahazel/lsat-scheduler/issues/191); [ADR 0004](../adr/0004-fill-drilling-days-to-a-fill-window.md) records the fill window.

## Record lifecycle

DrillMap saves one Monday–Sunday weekly schedule per student in the configured application timezone, initially `America/Chicago`. The deterministic `adaptive-weekly-schedule-v3` policy uses the student's study settings, the performance trend, the practice-test result of this week's weekly upload, and the student's earlier saved weekly schedules. Activity check-ins do not affect it. The schedule is created on the first visit to This week in its week. Repeat and concurrent visits return the same schedule. Later uploads and setting changes affect only a schedule that has not yet been created.

The saved record holds the policy version, generation time and inputs, source snapshot IDs, the practice-test result ID when one was used, day plans, daily capacities, and schedule reasons. Activity check-ins are separate, editable records. Students see their own saved schedule; the tutor's admin view reads the same schedule and reasons. Past v1 and v2 schedules remain readable without a backfill. Account deletion removes the student's analytics snapshots, practice-test results, weekly schedules, activity check-ins, and account together.

### Upload window

A weekly upload counts for a week by the date it is confirmed, in the schedule timezone. A confirmation from Monday through Friday counts for that week. A confirmation on Saturday or Sunday counts for the next week. A new schedule for week W needs at least one confirmed analytics snapshot that counts for W. Without one, This week shows the weekly upload instead of a plan.

### Analytics and practice-test inputs

An eligible analytics snapshot requires fresh, usable 7Sage LR and RC screenshots and student confirmation that the extraction matches and is the latest. The newest three eligible snapshots that count for W or an earlier week form the performance trend, weighted 60%, 30%, and 10%, with weights renormalized when fewer exist. DrillMap's RC passage types are Art, Humanities, Science, Law, and Comparative. 7Sage also lists other passage types, such as Critique or debate, Implied, and Single position; DrillMap ignores them. 7Sage leaves a passage type off the page when the student has answered few of its questions or already beats their goal on it, so a missing type has no accuracy (tutor rule, 2026-10-28). An RC screenshot may show a percentage for 0 to 5 passage types. The review keeps all 5 passage-type rows; an empty RC box shows no error and does not block approval, and a typed value must be from 0 to 100. The snapshot saves only the passage types that have a value. LR question-type accuracy and RC passage-type accuracy are weighted the same way. A missing row contributes no invented accuracy; ranking uses only snapshots that report it. The newest snapshot supplies restricted priority flags.

The practice-test result is the newest one that counts for W. It holds each section's 7Sage score and each LR question's result and tags. A missed question is wrong or unanswered. A hidden result counts as missed or correct when the section score settles it; otherwise the student picks which hidden questions were missed. Each question-stem tag on a missed LR question counts once. Logic-type tags, unknown tags, and RC sections do not count. When this week's weekly schedule had no practice test, the weekly upload saves no practice-test result.

## Study settings and weekly shape

- Students set capacity for each day in 15-minute steps, from 30 minutes through 8 hours, and choose a preferred practice-test day. The app stores capacity in minutes. No day may have zero capacity, and there is no default preferred day. Changes apply to the next schedule created.
- A normal week puts a full practice test on the preferred practice-test day and its practice-test review day on the following day. The usual rest day is Wednesday. If the practice test or its review takes Wednesday, rest moves to the day before the practice test. Remaining days are drilling days.
- A Sunday practice test puts its practice-test review day on Monday of the next weekly schedule. That schedule reads the preceding saved schedule to identify the carry-over, even if the student's preference has changed. If Monday is also the preferred practice-test day, that week places its practice test as if Tuesday were preferred. If the Monday review takes the rest day this produces, rest moves to the first free day from the schedule's creation onward.
- A week beginning within the calendar month before the goal test date, and before that date, is a near-test week. It schedules two LawHub practice tests three or four days apart, each followed by its practice-test review day. The second practice test falls on Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday when the preferred practice-test day is Monday through Sunday respectively. A second practice test that would fall on a Monday holding last week's practice-test review day is dropped.
- If the schedule is created after the preferred practice-test day, it contains no practice test that week. A schedule created on that day keeps the test. In a near-test week, a second practice test whose day has already passed is dropped, and the preferred-day test stays. Past non-rest days are unscheduled days, with no retroactive work; the computed rest day remains rest even if it has passed. Missed weeks are not backfilled.
- The practice test and its review each use the accommodation-adjusted full-test duration: 150 minutes at standard time, 187.5 at 1.5x, or 225 at 2x. Review holds no other activity. Practice-test days and practice-test review days have no fill window.

## Filling drilling days

The policy walks active drilling days in Monday–Sunday order. It keeps one LR stream and one RC stream across the week, so each day continues where the previous day stopped.

**Fill window.** A drilling day's planned total lands between its daily capacity minus 10 minutes and its daily capacity plus 30 minutes. A unit "fits" when it keeps the day at or below capacity plus 30 minutes. LR question and RC passage estimates include review time and scale by 1, 1.25, and 1.5 for standard, 1.5x, and 2x accommodation.

**Daily minimum.** Every drilling day without the full section receives a daily minimum: the next LR line or one RC passage, whichever subject is behind the week's drilling ratio of 10 LR questions per RC passage; ties choose LR. It is assigned even when it exceeds daily capacity. When the paired LR drill is due on that day and the daily minimum plus the pair would not fit, the paired LR drill becomes the daily minimum.

**Filling.** While the day is below its daily capacity, the engine adds the next LR line if it fits and LR is behind the drilling ratio or a 2-passage RC drill does not fit. Otherwise it adds a 2-passage RC drill if that fits. Otherwise it adds a 5-question line of the next LR type if that fits. It stops when nothing fits. The day alternates which subject appears first.

**LR lines.** The first pass gives each type in the week's LR type order one 5-question line. On a day that holds a paired LR drill, the pair's types are skipped, as Paired LR drill below states. After the first pass, each line is 10 questions of one type. No LR line holds 25 questions of one type; the full section is its own activity. First-pass lines use L2–4 below a current score of 165 and L3–5 at 165 or higher. Lines after the first pass use L3–5.

**LR type order.** Starred question types come first, weakest first. Restricted Stimulus Logic types flagged top priority in the newest snapshot come next. Other reported types follow, weakest to strongest, with rules-list order breaking ties. When the whole week cannot hold one line of every starred type, starred rotation orders starred types least recently scheduled first, then by weakness and rules-list order, and non-starred types wait. "Least recently scheduled" uses the student's saved weekly schedules: every scheduled line counts whether or not it was checked off, both types of a paired LR drill count, and a full section does not.

**Missed-type priority.** When the practice-test result has missed stem types, the type with the highest count goes first in the LR type order, ahead of the starred types and of starred rotation. Ties break by lower trend accuracy, then rules-list order. The week saves a `missed_type_priority` schedule reason: "<Type> comes first this week. You missed it most on your last practice test." Without a practice-test result, the order is unchanged. The priority lasts one week, because the next week uses its own upload's result.

**Paired LR drill.** One line of 10 questions across two types from the tutor's pair list: Strengthen + Weaken, Flaw or descriptive weakening + Weaken, Strengthen + Most strongly supported, Must be true + Must be false, Parallel flawed method of reasoning + Parallel method of reasoning, and Sufficient assumption + Necessary assumption. The quota is 2 per weekly schedule when the current score is 160 or higher, else 1, with at most one per day. The engine chooses the week's pairs before it fills days. The first pair holds the highest-ranked type in the week's LR type order, and that type is named first. At a quota of 2, the second pair holds the highest-ranked type in that order that is not in the first pair, named first, and it shares no type with the first pair. For example, after Flaw or descriptive weakening + Weaken, the second pair can be Sufficient + Necessary assumption, but never Strengthen + Weaken. When no such pair exists, the week holds one paired LR drill. This is tutor rule 3 from round 8 (2026-11-01); it replaces the earlier rule, where both pairs held the highest-ranked type and were usually the same pair. The engine places the pair on the first active drilling days where it fits, after the daily minimum or full section. It does not place the pair before the week's first LR line unless the pair holds the type that goes first. The pair uses L2–4 below a current score of 165 and L3–5 at 165 or higher. A drilling day that holds a paired LR drill holds no single-type LR line of either pair type, at any size (5 or 10 questions). That day takes the next type in the LR type order, or RC, instead. A skipped type keeps its place in the order and can come on a later day. This is tutor rule 2 from round 8 (2026-11-01); it replaces the earlier behavior, where a pair's day could also hold a line of a pair type.

**RC drills.** The daily minimum is 1 passage. Every other RC drill is 2 passages of two different passage types on one line. The RC order is trend passage-type accuracy, lowest first, with rules-list order (Art, Humanities, Science, Law, Comparative) breaking ties; a type without accuracy goes last. The first 5 passages walk that order once, so each type gets one passage. Later passages cycle through only the types with an accuracy in the newest approved snapshot, in the same order; when the newest snapshot has no RC accuracy, the full order repeats. A 2-passage drill takes the next two passages in that sequence. When the newest snapshot has only one type, a 2-passage drill after the first 5 passages holds that type twice (tutor rule, 2026-10-27). A 1-passage drill uses a level from L2 to L5 set by its place in the week's passage sequence. A 2-passage drill takes the next difficulty pair in turn: L2 and L4, L3 and L5, L3 and L4.

**Full section.** One full LR or RC section goes on the remaining drilling day with the most daily capacity, with earlier days winning ties. It alternates subject from the student's most recent saved full section, starting with LR when none exists. The full section satisfies that day's daily minimum, and the engine still fills the day to its fill window. It does not count toward the drilling ratio or starred coverage. Drop it only when the current score is below 150 or no remaining drilling day has at least 60 minutes of daily capacity.

**Capacity overage.** Each drilling, practice-test, and practice-test review day records the minutes by which its planned total exceeds its daily capacity. The engine writes no schedule reason for it, and the student sees no notice.

## Student-facing schedule

This week is a checklist. Each day header shows the day type, planned total, and daily capacity saved with that schedule, for example "Drilling · 1 h 37 min of 1 h 30 min". Each schedule activity is one line with its label and no planned minutes:

| Activity | Label |
| --- | --- |
| Full section | "1 Full LR Section" or "1 Full RC Section" |
| LR line | "10 Weaken (L3-5)" |
| Paired LR drill | "10 Necessary + Sufficient assumption (L2-4)". When both type names are two words with the same last word, the first drops it. |
| 1-passage RC drill | "1 RC Passage Law L3" |
| 2-passage RC drill | "2 RC Passages Art L2, Comparative L4". Each passage shows its own level, never a range. The easier passage comes first; passages with the same level keep their engine order. |

A student checks off a line with one tap and can undo it. An activity check-in is not started or completed; there is no skip and no actual minutes. The header counts "N of M done", and a finished day shows "All done". A correction changes the check-in, not the saved plan. Unscheduled days have a distinct dashed card. The rest day, a near-test week, a week without a practice test, and a practice-test review day carried from last week use plain labels.

On Saturday and Sunday, This week shows a reminder with a "Plan next week" link until next week's weekly upload is confirmed. Between that confirmation and Monday, This week shows the current plan with no reminder. When the week has no schedule and no upload that counts for it, This week opens the weekly upload. From Monday to Friday that upload is "Plan this week" and builds a weekly schedule from today to Sunday ([#214](https://github.com/Jahazel/lsat-scheduler/issues/214) replaces the earlier analytics-only page for this path).

The policy saves a schedule reason, with a code, week or day placement, and final sentence, for each policy departure at creation. v3 schedule reasons cover missed-type priority, starred rotation, a dropped full section, a moved rest day, no practice test, a near-test week, an unscheduled day, a practice-test review day carried over from last week's Sunday practice test, and a practice-test review day due next Monday. The `capacity_overage` and `skip_reduction` codes exist only on saved v1 and v2 schedules, and This week hides `capacity_overage`. The baseline drilling ratio and LR type focus get no schedule reason. Past sentences never change when policy wording changes. The admin view shows the same saved sentences.

## Weekly upload

The "Plan next week" page asks for the LR and RC analytics screenshots, four practice-test review screenshots (one per section), and the current score, prefilled from Study settings. The score must be a whole number from 120 to 180. After "Read my screenshots", "Check what we read" shows the analytics values, each practice-test section with its 7Sage score and missed count, the type that goes first, the current score, and a preview of next Monday's first drills. Confirming saves the analytics snapshot, the practice-test result, and the current score in one transaction. When this week's weekly schedule had no practice test, the page asks only for the analytics screenshots and next week has no missed-type priority.

From Monday to Friday, a student whose week has no weekly schedule gets the same page as "Plan this week". It asks for last week's practice test only when last week's weekly schedule had one. Its preview shows only today to Sunday, and "Confirm and build this week" saves the same three records and builds the rest of the week, with the most-missed type first. Once the week has a weekly schedule, the weekly upload stays closed until Saturday.

## Verification and release

Engine, service, repository, route, and component tests cover deterministic placement, the fill window for every capacity from 30 to 480 minutes and every accommodation, paired LR drill quota and placement, RC pairing and order, missed-type priority, analytics weighting, the upload window, saved inputs and reasons, v1 and v2 display, access control, and concurrent creation. Run `npm test`, `npm run lint`, and `npm run build` for the implementation gate.

## Scope limits

Scheduling remains deterministic. The app uses Claude only to read screenshots, which the student checks before they count; it does not use an LLM to create, modify, explain, or review a weekly schedule. Official LSAT attempts, RC question-type tags, and logic-type tags do not affect scheduling. The app does not scrape 7Sage, store screenshot images, read the scaled practice-test score, backfill missed weeks, or offer an editable scheduling policy or timezone.
