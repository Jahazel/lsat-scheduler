# Landing page

Entry points: a signed-out visit to `/`, and a signed-in visit to `/`.

- `landing-sections`: open `/` signed out at 1440, 768 and 390 px wide. Expect the `Your practice results, turned into next week's plan` heading in balanced lines (three at each width), no proof numbers, no tab list and no `7Sage` in the hero, the `Pricing` region with `id="pricing"`, the `Weekly` and `Monthly` plan cards, the cancel line, and the `Start` region with the `Get next week's plan from your own analytics` closing band. Require `document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= innerWidth` at each width. At 390 px the plan cards stack in one column.
- `landing-section-grid`: at 1440, 768 and 390 px, `.section-guides` starts at the hero section's bottom and ends at the footer's top. It is 1120 px wide at 1440 (left 160), 720 px at 768 (left 24) and the 350 px content width at 390 (left 20), with five `repeating-linear-gradient` layers at `0, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%`, or two at 390. The four `.section-band` gaps are 128 px tall (56 px at 390), and each `::after` connector sits at `left: 50%` with an ink at 0.4 dash. The four `main h2` labels read `[02]How it works`, `[03]Pricing`, `[04]FAQ` and `[05]Start` in 13/20 px Geist Mono, as wide as the guides, with a dashed bottom border, a `::before` solid rule as wide as the window, and an `aria-hidden` fog bracket number. The accessibility snapshot names each heading without its number.
- `landing-start`: every `main >> role=link[name="Start free week"]` has `href="/signup"`. Click the first one and expect the `Sign up for DrillMap` heading.
- `landing-prices`: the Monthly card shows `Best value · about 28% less` and `About $34.60 a month on Weekly`. Both come from `src/lib/planPrices.ts`; a long-price check edits `PLAN_PRICES` there in the working tree only, reloads, and reverts the edit.
- `landing-hero-cards`: the `Sample plan cards` group shows six cards at 1440 px, four at 768 px (two top corners, two on the screen edges) and three in a sideways scroll-snap row at 390 px. At 768 px the hero button's bottom sits above the lower cards' top. Read each `.hero-card-drift` computed `opacity` and `transform` every 120 ms from load: left cards start at `translate(-40px, 16px)`, right cards at `translate(40px, 16px)`, scale 0.96, 60 ms apart, settled by about 1.1 s. `.hero-card-float` then moves up to `-4px` on a 6 s loop; its `animationPlayState` is `paused` after scrolling the hero off screen and `running` back at the top. At 768 px and up, after scrolling to 400 px, each `.hero-card-depth` has a different computed `translate` and `rotate`. Move the mouse 60 px beside the first card: its `[data-face]` gets an inline `translate(...) rotate(...) scale(1.00…)` and a `--near` above 0; move away and it returns to `translate(0px, 0px) rotate(0deg) scale(1)`. At 390 px, scroll the row to each card: the widened (18 px) dot follows. With `page.emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: "reduce" })` the three animation names are `none`, the face gets no inline transform, the dot's `transition-property` is `none`, and the button has no scale transition. In a `deviceScaleFactor: 2` context at 1440 px the hero images request `w=3840` and serve the 2880 px export. The screen images are served from `public/landing/`; a failed `/_next/image` request there means the proxy matcher stopped skipping that folder's `.png` files. A signed-out request for a non-image path under `/landing/`, such as `/landing/pricing`, still redirects to `/login`.
- `landing-how-it-works`: at each width, the `How it works` region (`id="how-it-works"`) lists four steps, `1 Tell us about your prep` to `4 Repeat every weekend`. Each `[data-testid="step-screen"]` shows `public/landing/step-<n>-*.png`, with the `-phone` image below 768 px. On a fresh load at the top, screens below the fold have an inline `clip-path: inset(100% …)`; scroll one to mid-window and read its computed `clip-path` about 150 ms later (mid-wipe), then after 700 ms (`inset(-40px …)`); scrolling away and back does not replay it. Clicking `Pricing` in the top bar from the top shows every screen with no inline `clip-path` and no transition, and lands `#pricing` at `top: 64`. Clicking `How it works` lands `#how-it-works` at `top: 64` and scrolls smoothly (read `scrollY` about 120 ms after the click). With `page.emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: "reduce" })` no screen is hidden and the jump is instant. A context with `javaScriptEnabled: false` shows all four screens.
- `landing-pricing`: at 1280, 768 and 390 px, the two `#pricing article` plans share the guides' left edge and width: two halves of the guides at 1280 and 768, stacked full width at 390. The prices' computed `font-size` is 56 px at 1280 and 48 px below 1024; the Monthly badge is ink with chalk text. On a fresh load at the top, `#pricing` has `data-reveal="waiting"` and every `.reveal-item` has opacity 0 and `translateY(16px)`; scroll it into view and after about 1.2 s they are opacity 1 with no transform and `.spectrum-key::after` is `scaleX(1)`; scrolling away and back does not replay it. Hover the Weekly `.plan-card` with the mouse: computed transform `translateY(-2px)`, border darker than `vellum-line` (oklab L about 0.857), `::after` opacity 1. With `page.emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: "reduce" })` the items wait with no transform, fade with `opacity 0.12s`, the underline is never scaled, and the hover keeps the border and shadow with no rise. With CDP `Emulation.setTouchEmulationEnabled` the hover changes nothing.
- `landing-questions`: at each width, the `FAQ` region lists five heading buttons, `Do I need a card to start?` to `Is the plan made by AI?`, with `Which practice sites work?` fourth; each row shows an `aria-hidden` mono `Q01`–`Q05` that is not in the button's name, dashed rules between rows, and a 32 px square icon. Only the first has `aria-expanded="true"`, its icon is ink with a chalk minus, and only its answer is in the accessibility snapshot. At 1440 the `FAQ` title sits on guide column 1 (left 160, 280 px wide) with computed `position: sticky`, stays at `top: 112` after scrolling 250 px into the section, and the list spans columns 2–4 (left 440, 840 px wide); at 768 and 390 the title is static above the list. Row text is 20/28 px at 1440, 18/26 at 768 and 17/24 at 390. On a fresh load the section has `data-reveal="waiting"` and its `.reveal-item`s are opacity 0 and `translateY(16px)`; at 20% in view they settle, the rows with `--reveal-delay` 80 ms to 320 ms in 60 ms steps. Hover a closed row with the mouse: question text smoke (`rgb(92, 92, 92)`), icon border darker than `vellum-line` (oklab L about 0.857). Click `Which practice sites work?`: both stay open and it shows `7Sage today, with more sites coming. You can also type your numbers by hand.` About 80 ms after the click the answer is still short of its full height and opacity; after 300 ms it is full and `visibility: visible`, the plus's vertical bar is `scaleY(0)` and the icon is solid. Close the first: its answer is `0` px tall and `visibility: hidden`. With `page.emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: "reduce" })` the reveal items wait with no transform and fade with `opacity 0.12s`, the answer's computed transition is only `opacity 0.12s …` and `visibility`, the bar's transition is `none`, and the icon still turns solid. A context with `javaScriptEnabled: false` shows all five questions and the first answer. For the long-data case, add three sample questions in `src/app/page.tsx` and set `PLAN_PRICES` to 1299 / 3999 in the working tree only, open a second question, take shots, and revert.
- `landing-signed-in`: seed a student, sign in with `signInAs`, open `/`, and expect `/dashboard`.
