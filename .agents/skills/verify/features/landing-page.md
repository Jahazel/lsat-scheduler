# Landing page

Entry points: a signed-out visit to `/`, and a signed-in visit to `/`.

- `landing-sections`: open `/` signed out at 1440, 768 and 390 px wide. Expect the `Your practice results, turned into next week's plan` heading in balanced lines (three at each width), no proof numbers, no tab list and no `7Sage` in the hero, the `Pricing` region with `id="pricing"`, the `Weekly` and `Monthly` plan cards, the cancel line, and the `Start` region with the `Get next week's plan from your own analytics` closing band. Require `document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= innerWidth` at each width. At 390 px the plan cards stack in one column.
- `landing-section-grid`: at 1440, 768 and 390 px, `.section-guides` starts at the hero section's bottom and ends at the footer's top. It is 1120 px wide at 1440 (left 160), 720 px at 768 (left 24) and the 350 px content width at 390 (left 20), with five `repeating-linear-gradient` layers at `0, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%`, or two at 390. The four `.section-band` gaps are 128 px tall (56 px at 390), and each `::after` connector sits at `left: 50%` with an ink at 0.4 dash. The four `main h2` labels read `[02]How it works`, `[03]Pricing`, `[04]FAQ` and `[05]Start` in 13/20 px Geist Mono, as wide as the guides, with a dashed bottom border, a `::before` solid rule as wide as the window, and an `aria-hidden` fog bracket number. The accessibility snapshot names each heading without its number.
- `landing-start`: every `main >> role=link[name="Start free week"]` has `href="/signup"`. Click the first one and expect the `Sign up for DrillMap` heading.
- `landing-prices`: the Monthly card shows `Best value · about 31% less` and `About $43.30 a month on Weekly`. Both come from `src/lib/planPrices.ts`; a long-price check edits `PLAN_PRICES` there in the working tree only, reloads, and reverts the edit.
- `landing-hero-cards`: the `Sample plan cards` group shows six cards from 1360 px (check 1360 and 1440), four from 768 to 1359 px (two top corners, two on the screen edges; check 768, 1024, 1152, 1279 and 1359) and three in a sideways scroll-snap row at 390 px. At every one of those widths from 768 up, each visible `[data-face]` box sits inside `0..innerWidth`, `document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= innerWidth`, and from 1024 to 1359 px no card overlaps the hero `h1` or the `Start free week` button. At 768 px the hero button's bottom sits above the lower cards' top. Read each `.hero-card-drift` computed `opacity` and `transform` every 120 ms from load: left cards start at `translate(-40px, 16px)`, right cards at `translate(40px, 16px)`, scale 0.96, 60 ms apart, settled by about 1.1 s. `.hero-card-float` then moves up to `-4px` on a 6 s loop; its `animationPlayState` is `paused` after scrolling the hero off screen and `running` back at the top. At 768 px and up (check 1024 too), each `.hero-card-scroll` moves only up, with `rotate: none`: after scrolling to 400 px its computed `translate` is `0px` and half its inline `--scroll-rise` (35% of the card's rise, -21 px for Monday), and from 800 px on the full `--scroll-rise`. Move the mouse 60 px beside the first card: its `[data-face]` gets an inline `translate(...) rotate(...) scale(1.00…)` and a `--near` above 0; move away and it returns to `translate(0px, 0px) rotate(0deg) scale(1)`. At 390 px, scroll the row to each card: the widened (18 px) dot follows. With `page.emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: "reduce" })` the three animation names are `none`, the face gets no inline transform, the dot's `transition-property` is `none`, and the button has no scale transition. In a `deviceScaleFactor: 2` context at 1440 px the hero images request `w=3840` and serve the 2880 px export. The screen images are served from `public/landing/`; a failed `/_next/image` request there means the proxy matcher stopped skipping that folder's `.png` files. A signed-out request for a non-image path under `/landing/`, such as `/landing/pricing`, still redirects to `/login`.
- `landing-how-it-works`: at each width, the `How it works` region (`id="how-it-works"`) exposes one ordered list of four steps, `1 Tell us about your prep`, `2 Upload your practice analytics`, `3 Get your week`, `4 Repeat every weekend`, and four buttons named `Go to step <n>: <title>`. Below 768 px the list is the visible stack of color panels with the `-phone` screens and `[data-testid="how-track"]` is `display: none`. At 768 px and up the list is visually hidden and the pinned block shows instead: scroll so `[data-testid="how-pin"]` is stuck (its top equals its computed `top`), then move through the track's run (track height minus pin height) in quarters. Each quarter sets `data-state="on"` on one `.how-step`, `.how-shot` and `.how-mat`, the `Step 0<n> / 04` count, and `aria-current="step"` on its button; earlier ones read `before`, later ones `after`, and scrolling back reverses them. A `Go to step n` click scrolls the pin first, so `aria-current` moves about a second later; wait for it rather than reading it right after the click. About 150 ms into a swap the new `.how-step` and `.how-shot` are mid-fade and mid-rise. The current button's inner fill has an inline `scaleX` that follows the scroll. Clicking a button scrolls smoothly (read `scrollY` about 120 ms after) to that step's start. The pin's top must sit at or below the top bar's bottom (65 px) and its bottom at or above the window bottom at 768×640, 768×760, 768×820, 1280×680 and 1440×900; the tablet screen is 672 px wide and 190, 302 and 362 px tall at those three tablet heights. In a `deviceScaleFactor: 2` context at 1440 px each `.how-shot img` serves the 1356 px export (`naturalWidth` 440 at `w=1920`). With `page.emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: "reduce" })` the on-state transitions are `0.12s` with no delay and no transform, the key-phrase underline has `transform: none`, and a button jump lands at once. Clicking `How it works` and `Pricing` in the top bar lands each section at `top: 64`. A context with `javaScriptEnabled: false` hides the track and shows the list with all four desktop screens at 768 px and up. Every step screen loads eagerly, and each sits in a `<picture>` whose `data:` source keeps it from downloading where it is hidden: below 768 px the page requests only the four `-phone` screens, and from 768 px up only the four desktop ones, even after scrolling to the bottom. Reloading with the scroll restored anywhere in the section prints no Next LCP warning. The accessibility snapshot names the section `How it works` only in the region and its heading.
- `landing-pricing`: at 1280, 768 and 390 px, the two `#pricing article` plans share the guides' left edge and width: two halves of the guides at 1280 and 768, stacked full width at 390. The prices' computed `font-size` is 56 px at 1280 and 48 px below 1024; the Monthly badge is ink with chalk text. On a fresh load at the top, `#pricing` has `data-reveal="waiting"` and every `.reveal-item` has opacity 0 and `translateY(16px)`; scroll it into view and after about 1.2 s they are opacity 1 with no transform and `.spectrum-key::after` is `scaleX(1)`; scrolling away and back does not replay it. Hover the Weekly `.plan-card` with the mouse: computed transform `translateY(-2px)`, border darker than `vellum-line` (oklab L about 0.857), `::after` opacity 1. With `page.emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: "reduce" })` the items wait with no transform, fade with `opacity 0.12s`, the underline is never scaled, and the hover keeps the border and shadow with no rise. With CDP `Emulation.setTouchEmulationEnabled` the hover changes nothing.
- `landing-questions`: at each width, the `FAQ` region lists nine heading buttons, `How is the study schedule made?` to `Is the plan made by AI?`, with `Can I cancel?` sixth; each row shows an `aria-hidden` mono `Q01`–`Q09` that is not in the button's name, dashed rules between rows, and a 32 px square icon. On load, and again after a reload, every row has `aria-expanded="false"` and no answer is in the accessibility snapshot. At 1440 the `FAQ` title sits on guide column 1 (left 160, 280 px wide) with computed `position: sticky`, stays at `top: 112` after scrolling 250 px into the section, and the list spans columns 2–4 (left 440, 840 px wide); at 768 and 390 the title is static above the list. Row text is 20/28 px at 1440, 18/26 at 768 and 17/24 at 390. On a fresh load the section has `data-reveal="waiting"` and its `.reveal-item`s are opacity 0 and `translateY(16px)`; at 20% in view they settle, the rows with `--reveal-delay` 80 ms to 560 ms in 60 ms steps. Hover a closed row with the mouse: question text smoke (`rgb(92, 92, 92)`), icon border darker than `vellum-line` (oklab L about 0.857). Click `How is the study schedule made?`, then `Does this replace a tutor or a course?`: the first closes, only the second has `aria-expanded="true"`, its icon is ink with a chalk minus, and its answer shows two paragraphs, `No, it works alongside them.…` and `DrillMap gives you the kind of weekly structure a tutor would.…` About 80 ms after the click the answer is still short of its full height and opacity; after 300 ms it is full and `visibility: visible`, the plus's vertical bar is `scaleY(0)` and the icon is solid. The `How is the study schedule made?` answer is `0` px tall and `visibility: hidden`. Click the second again: every row is closed. With `page.emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: "reduce" })` the reveal items wait with no transform and fade with `opacity 0.12s`, the answer's computed transition is only `opacity 0.12s …` and `visibility`, the bar's transition is `none`, and the icon still turns solid. A context with `javaScriptEnabled: false` shows all nine questions with every answer closed. For the long-data case, add three sample questions in `src/app/page.tsx` and set `PLAN_PRICES` to 1299 / 3999 in the working tree only, open one question, take shots, and revert.
