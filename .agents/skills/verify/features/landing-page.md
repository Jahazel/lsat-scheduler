# Landing page

Entry points: a signed-out visit to `/`, and a signed-in visit to `/`.

- `landing-sections`: open `/` signed out at 1440, 768 and 390 px wide. Expect the `Your practice results, turned into next week's plan` heading in balanced lines (three at each width), no proof numbers, no tab list and no `7Sage` in the hero, the `Pricing` region with `id="pricing"`, the `Weekly` and `Monthly` plan cards, the cancel line, and the `Start` region with the `Get next week's plan from your own analytics` closing band. Require `document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= innerWidth` at each width. At 390 px the plan cards stack in one column.
- `landing-section-grid`: at 1440, 768 and 390 px, `.section-guides` starts at the hero section's bottom and ends at the footer's top. It is 1120 px wide at 1440 (left 160), 720 px at 768 (left 24) and the 350 px content width at 390 (left 20), with five `repeating-linear-gradient` layers at `0, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%`, or two at 390. The four `.section-band` gaps are 128 px tall (56 px at 390), and each `::after` connector sits at `left: 50%` with an ink at 0.4 dash. The four `main h2` labels read `[02]How it works`, `[03]Pricing`, `[04]FAQ` and `[05]Start` in 13/20 px Geist Mono, as wide as the guides, with a dashed bottom border, a `::before` solid rule as wide as the window, and an `aria-hidden` fog bracket number. The accessibility snapshot names each heading without its number.
- `landing-start`: every `main >> role=link[name="Start free week"]` has `href="/signup"`. Click the first one and expect the `Sign up for DrillMap` heading.
- `landing-prices`: the Monthly card shows `Best value · about 28% less` and `About $34.60 a month on Weekly`. Both come from `src/lib/planPrices.ts`; a long-price check edits `PLAN_PRICES` there in the working tree only, reloads, and reverts the edit.
- `landing-hero-cards`: the `Sample plan cards` group shows six cards at 1440 px, four at 768 px (two top corners, two on the screen edges) and three in a sideways scroll-snap row at 390 px. At 768 px the hero button's bottom sits above the lower cards' top. Read each `.hero-card-drift` computed `opacity` and `transform` every 120 ms from load: left cards start at `translate(-40px, 16px)`, right cards at `translate(40px, 16px)`, scale 0.96, 60 ms apart, settled by about 1.1 s. `.hero-card-float` then moves up to `-4px` on a 6 s loop; its `animationPlayState` is `paused` after scrolling the hero off screen and `running` back at the top. At 768 px and up, after scrolling to 400 px, each `.hero-card-depth` has a different computed `translate` and `rotate`. Move the mouse 60 px beside the first card: its `[data-face]` gets an inline `translate(...) rotate(...) scale(1.00…)` and a `--near` above 0; move away and it returns to `translate(0px, 0px) rotate(0deg) scale(1)`. At 390 px, scroll the row to each card: the widened (18 px) dot follows. With `page.emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: "reduce" })` the three animation names are `none`, the face gets no inline transform, the dot's `transition-property` is `none`, and the button has no scale transition. In a `deviceScaleFactor: 2` context at 1440 px the hero images request `w=3840` and serve the 2880 px export. The screen images are served from `public/landing/`; a failed `/_next/image` request there means the proxy matcher stopped skipping that folder's `.png` files. A signed-out request for a non-image path under `/landing/`, such as `/landing/pricing`, still redirects to `/login`.
- `landing-how-it-works`: at each width, the `How it works` region (`id="how-it-works"`) exposes one ordered list of four steps, `1 Tell us about your prep`, `2 Upload your practice analytics`, `3 Get your week`, `4 Repeat every weekend`, and four buttons named `Go to step <n>: <title>`. Below 768 px the list is the visible stack of color panels with the `-phone` screens and `[data-testid="how-track"]` is `display: none`. At 768 px and up the list is visually hidden and the pinned block shows instead: scroll so `[data-testid="how-pin"]` is stuck (its top equals its computed `top`), then move through the track's run (track height minus pin height) in quarters. Each quarter sets `data-state="on"` on one `.how-step`, `.how-shot` and `.how-mat`, the `Step 0<n> / 04` count, and `aria-current="step"` on its button; earlier ones read `before`, later ones `after`, and scrolling back reverses them. About 150 ms into a swap the new `.how-step` and `.how-shot` are mid-fade and mid-rise. The current button's inner fill has an inline `scaleX` that follows the scroll. Clicking a button scrolls smoothly (read `scrollY` about 120 ms after) to that step's start. The pin's top must sit at or below the top bar's bottom (65 px) and its bottom at or above the window bottom at 768×640, 768×760, 768×820, 1280×680 and 1440×900; the tablet screen is 672 px wide and 190, 302 and 362 px tall at those three tablet heights. In a `deviceScaleFactor: 2` context at 1440 px each `.how-shot img` serves the 1356 px export (`naturalWidth` 440 at `w=1920`). With `page.emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: "reduce" })` the on-state transitions are `0.12s` with no delay and no transform, the key-phrase underline has `transform: none`, and a button jump lands at once. Clicking `How it works` and `Pricing` in the top bar lands each section at `top: 64`. A context with `javaScriptEnabled: false` hides the track and shows the list with all four desktop screens at 768 px and up.
- `landing-questions`: at each width, the `FAQ` region lists five heading buttons, `Do I need a card to start?` to `Is the plan made by AI?`; only the first has `aria-expanded="true"` and only its answer is in the accessibility snapshot. Click `Do I need 7Sage?`: both stay open. About 80 ms after the click the answer is still short of its full height and opacity; after 300 ms it is full and `visibility: visible`, and the plus's vertical bar is `scaleY(0)`. Close the first: its answer is `0` px tall and `visibility: hidden`. With `page.emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: "reduce" })` the answer's computed transition is only `opacity 0.12s …` and `visibility`, so the height changes at once. A context with `javaScriptEnabled: false` shows all five questions and the first answer. For the long-data case, add three sample questions in `src/app/page.tsx` and set `PLAN_PRICES` to 1299 / 3999 in the working tree only, open a second question, take shots, and revert.
- `landing-signed-in`: seed a student, sign in with `signInAs`, open `/`, and expect `/dashboard`.

For PR pictures, scroll each section into view and take a screen-sized `page.screenshot`.
