# Landing page

Entry points: a signed-out visit to `/`, and a signed-in visit to `/`.

- `landing-sections`: open `/` signed out at 1440, 768 and 390 px wide. Expect the `Your practice results, turned into next week's plan` heading in balanced lines (three at each width), no proof numbers, no tab list and no `7Sage` in the hero, the `Pricing` region with `id="pricing"`, the `Weekly` and `Monthly` plan cards, the cancel line, and the `Start` region with the `Get next week's plan from your own analytics` closing band. Require `document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= innerWidth` at each width. At 390 px the plan cards stack in one column.
- `landing-section-grid`: at 320, 390, 768, 1024, 1440 and 2560 px, `.line-grid` runs from the top bar's bottom (64) to the footer's top, and `.line-grid-dots` fills it, hero included. `.line-grid-edges` is 1120 px wide at 1440 (left 160), 720 px at 768 (left 24) and the 350 px content width at 390 (left 20); its dashed layers sit at `0, 25%, 75%, 100%`, or `0, 100%` below 768 px. `.line-grid-middle` starts at the hero section's bottom with a 160 px mask fade, and has no image below 768 px. The dots sit on the lines: in a `GridGap` (the 128 px gap before `#pricing`, 56 px below 768), screenshot the gap with `.line-grid-lines` hidden, then with `.line-grid-dots` hidden, and compare the darkest pixel columns; each line has a dot column within 1 px. Repeat at 200% zoom (a 720 or 384 px viewport with `deviceScaleFactor: 2`). The four `main h2` labels read `[02]How it works`, `[03]Pricing`, `[04]FAQ` and `[05]Start` in 13/20 px Geist Mono, as wide as the guides, between a dashed `.line-grid-rule` as wide as the window and one as wide as the label, with an `aria-hidden` fog bracket number. The accessibility snapshot names each heading without its number. Worst case: set the Pricing label's text to a sentence of about 100 characters; at 390 px it wraps, and every line starts at the text's left (58), right of the number (20). The grid never moves on scroll.
- `landing-start`: every `main >> role=link[name="Start free week"]` has `href="/signup"`. Click the first one and expect the `Sign up for DrillMap` heading.
- `landing-prices`: the Monthly card shows `Best value · about 31% less` and `About $43.30 a month on Weekly`. Both come from `src/lib/planPrices.ts`; a long-price check edits `PLAN_PRICES` there in the working tree only, reloads, and reverts the edit.
- `landing-hero-cards`: the `Sample plan cards` group shows six cards from 1360 px (check 1360 and 1440), four from 768 to 1359 px (two top corners, two on the screen edges; check 768, 1024, 1152, 1279 and 1359) and three in a sideways scroll-snap row at 390 px. At every one of those widths from 768 up, each visible `[data-face]` box sits inside `0..innerWidth`, `document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= innerWidth`, and from 1024 to 1359 px no card overlaps the hero `h1` or the `Start free week` button. At 768 px the hero button's bottom sits above the lower cards' top. Read each `.hero-card-drift` computed `opacity` and `transform` every 120 ms from load: left cards start at `translate(-40px, 16px)`, right cards at `translate(40px, 16px)`, scale 0.96, 60 ms apart, settled by about 1.1 s. `.hero-card-float` then moves up to `-4px` on a 6 s loop; its `animationPlayState` is `paused` after scrolling the hero off screen and `running` back at the top. At 768 px and up (check 1024 too), each `.hero-card-scroll` moves only up, with `rotate: none`: after scrolling to 400 px its computed `translate` is `0px` and half its inline `--scroll-rise` (35% of the card's rise, -21 px for Monday), and from 800 px on the full `--scroll-rise`. Move the mouse 60 px beside the first card: its `[data-face]` gets an inline `translate(...) rotate(...) scale(1.00…)` and a `--near` above 0; move away and it returns to `translate(0px, 0px) rotate(0deg) scale(1)`. At 390 px, scroll the row to each card: the widened (18 px) dot follows. With `page.emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: "reduce" })` the three animation names are `none`, the face gets no inline transform, the dot's `transition-property` is `none`, and the button has no scale transition. In a `deviceScaleFactor: 2` context at 1440 px the hero images request `w=3840` and serve the 2880 px export. The screen images are served from `public/landing/`; a failed `/_next/image` request there means the proxy matcher stopped skipping that folder's `.png` files. A signed-out request for a non-image path under `/landing/`, such as `/landing/pricing`, still redirects to `/login`.
- `landing-how-it-works`: at each width, the `How it works` region (`id="how-it-works"`) exposes one ordered list of four steps, `1 Tell us about your prep`, `2 Upload your practice analytics`, `3 Get your week`, `4 Repeat every weekend`, and four buttons named `Go to step <n>: <title>`. The pin shows only from 1024 px wide, or from 768 px wide with a window at least 800 px tall (check 1440×900, 1024×700, 768×1024 and 768×800). Otherwise (check 768×799, 767×1200, 390×844 and 320×640) the list is the visible stack of Abyss Blue to Silver Mist panels, the screens below 768 px are the `-phone` ones, and `[data-testid="how-track"]` is `display: none`; on a fresh load every `ol > li` has `data-reveal="waiting"`, and scrolling one into view sets `shown` with its `.how-in` at opacity 1. Where it pins, the list is visually hidden and the pinned block shows instead. The track's run (track height minus pin height) is about 1.25 window heights: scroll so `[data-testid="how-pin"]` is stuck (its top equals its computed `top`), then move through the track's run (track height minus pin height) in quarters. Each quarter sets `data-state="on"` on one `.how-step`, `.how-shot` and `.how-mat`, the `Step 0<n> / 04` count, and `aria-current="step"` on its button; earlier ones read `before`, later ones `after`, and scrolling back reverses them. A `Go to step n` click scrolls the pin first, so `aria-current` moves about a second later; wait for it rather than reading it right after the click. About 150 ms into a swap the new `.how-step` and `.how-shot` are mid-fade and mid-rise. The current button's inner fill has an inline `scaleX` that follows the scroll. Clicking a button scrolls smoothly (read `scrollY` about 120 ms after) to that step's start. The pin's top must sit at or below the top bar's bottom (64 px) and its bottom at or above the window bottom at 768×800, 768×1024, 1024×700 and 1440×900. The pinned step title is 24 px from 1024 to 1279 px wide and 28 px elsewhere. In a `deviceScaleFactor: 2` context at 1440 px each `.how-shot img` serves the 1356 px export (`naturalWidth` 440 at `w=1920`). With `page.emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: "reduce" })` the on-state transitions are `0.12s` with no delay and no transform, the key-phrase underline has `transform: none`, and a button jump lands at once; on the stacked list the waiting items have no transform and the underline is `none`. Clicking `How it works` and `Pricing` in the top bar lands each section at `top: 64`. A context with `javaScriptEnabled: false` hides the track and shows the list with all four desktop screens at 768 px and up. The top bar `How it works` link at 768×700 shows the list's intro and step 1 at once (no `data-reveal`). Every step screen loads eagerly, and each sits in a `<picture>` whose `data:` source keeps it from downloading where it is hidden: below 768 px the page requests only the four `-phone` screens, and from 768 px up only the four desktop ones, even after scrolling to the bottom. Reloading with the scroll restored anywhere in the section prints no Next LCP warning. The accessibility snapshot names the section `How it works` only in the region and its heading.
- `landing-pricing`: at 1280, 768 and 390 px, the two `#pricing article` plans share the guides' left edge and width: two halves of the guides at 1280 and 768, stacked full width at 390.
