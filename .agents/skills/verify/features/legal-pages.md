# Legal pages

Entry points: `/legal/terms`, `/legal/privacy` and `/legal/ai-disclosure`, opened signed out and signed in, directly, with a section link (`#<id>`), from the footer's `Legal` links, from the page tabs, and from the AI disclosure's `Related` list. One layout renders all three: `src/components/legal/LegalPage.tsx`, with the section list in `OnThisPage.tsx`, the copy-link buttons in `SectionLinks.tsx`, and the `Related` list in `RelatedSections.tsx`. Each page's approved text and section ids live in `src/app/legal/<page>/`. The motion values come from `docs/design/research/legal-pages/motion.md`.

Run every check at 1440×900, 768×1024 and 390×844, once signed out and once as a seeded student (`signInAs`). Use Playwright with `drive`: one session per sign-in state, `page.setViewportSize` per width. Wait for `networkidle` after each `goto`. For a scroll, wait until `scrollY` stops changing; a smooth scroll is still moving 150 ms after a click.

The header's bottom edge, `[data-site-header]`'s `getBoundingClientRect().bottom`, sets where a section heading lands. It is 64 signed out, 73 signed in at 768 and up, and 57 signed in at 390. A heading lands 32 px under it at 1024 px and up, and 64 px under it below 1024, where the 48 px section bar sits between. So the heading `h2` top is 96, 128 and 128 signed out at 1440, 768 and 390, and 105, 137 and 121 signed in.

- `legal-header`: on each page, expect the `Legal` label, the `h1` (`Terms of Service`, `Privacy Policy`, `How DrillMap uses AI`), the date line (`Effective date: November 9, 2026` on Terms, `Last updated: November 10, 2026` on the other two), and the `Legal pages` navigation with three links, `Terms`, `Privacy` and `AI disclosure`. Only the current page's link has `aria-current="page"`. At 390 px that link sits inside the tab row's visible box, and `document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= innerWidth` at every width.
- `legal-open-at-section`: open `/legal/terms#cancelling-and-refunds` with `goto`. Once the scroll settles, the `Cancelling and refunds` heading's top is at the number above, and `document.activeElement` is that `h2`. At 1440 the `On this page` navigation's `[aria-current="location"]` link is `Cancelling and refunds`. Below 1024 the section bar's button reads `On this page Cancelling and refunds`. Then open `/legal/terms#unknown` from another path, such as `/legal/privacy`: expect `scrollY` 0, `About these Terms` marked, and no page or console error. A `goto` that changes only the hash is a same-page jump and keeps the old scroll, so it does not test this.
- `legal-list-desktop` (1024 px and up): the section bar's button is hidden. In the `On this page` navigation, click `7Sage and LSAC`. `scrollY` is still rising 150 ms after the click, then the heading lands at the number above. The address ends in `#7sage-and-lsac`, focus is on the heading, and the link is marked. The marker is the `span[aria-hidden]` beside the list. Its top and height equal the marked link's, 30 px for one line. Click `Disclaimers and limits on liability`, a two-line item: the marker grows to the item's 48 px. Click `Contact`: the page cannot scroll that heading up to the line, and `Contact` stays marked. Then scroll up with `page.mouse.wheel`: the mark moves to the section being read. Pick a section from the middle of the page, such as `7Sage and LSAC`, for the landing check. `Governing law` and later sections are too near the end to reach the line.
- `legal-section-bar` (below 1024 px): the `On this page` navigation is hidden. At the top of the page, the bar sits under the page tabs. After a jump it sticks with its top at the header's bottom edge. Click the bar's button (`aria-controls="on-this-page-list"`). Expect `aria-expanded="true"` and a visible `#on-this-page-list`. Click `7Sage and LSAC` in the list. The list closes, and the page scrolls smoothly to the heading at the number above. The address ends in `#7sage-and-lsac`, focus is on the heading, and the bar reads `7Sage and LSAC`. Open the list again and click 12 px under the panel's bottom edge: it closes. Do not click near the bottom of the window, because the signed-in tab bar is there and a click opens `Progress`. Open it again and press `Escape`: it closes, and focus returns to the bar's button.
- `legal-copy-link`: headless Chromium has a fine pointer, so each copy button shows only on hover. Grant `clipboard-read` and `clipboard-write` for `http://127.0.0.1:3100`. A `newSession` has no `url`, so write the address out. Hover the `Cancelling and refunds` heading, then click `Copy link to Cancelling and refunds`. Wait up to 1 s for `Copied`; it shows only after the clipboard write finishes. Then expect the page's `role="status"` to read `Link copied`, the address to end in `#cancelling-and-refunds`, and `navigator.clipboard.readText()` to return `http://127.0.0.1:3100/legal/terms#cancelling-and-refunds`. After 1.6 s `Copied` is gone.
- `legal-related`: on `/legal/ai-disclosure` there is no `On this page` list. The `Related` navigation has two links: `Terms` / `AI reads your screenshots` to `/legal/terms#ai-reads-your-screenshots`, and `Privacy` / `Your 7Sage screenshots` to `/legal/privacy#your-7sage-screenshots`. At 1440 the list sits right of the text; below 1024 it sits below the text. Click each link. That page opens with the heading at the number above and the section marked. Press Back: `/legal/ai-disclosure` again.
- `legal-back`: at 1440, open `/` signed out, or `/dashboard` signed in (a seeded student lands on `/dashboard/upload`). Click `Terms of Service` in the footer's `Legal` navigation (`exact: true`; the page tabs are `Legal pages`). Wait for the heading, then press Back: the URL is the page you started on.
- `legal-reduced-motion`: call `page.emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: "reduce" })` on `/legal/terms` at 1440. Click `7Sage and LSAC` from inside `page.evaluate`, and read `scrollY` in the same call: it already equals the final scroll. The marker's computed `transitionProperty` is `none`. At 390, `#on-this-page-list` has `transitionProperty: none` and opacity `1` right after the bar's click.
