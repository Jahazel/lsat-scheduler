# Admin

An Admin views students' saved schedules without editing them. Their own student side has the Walkthrough: a forward-only Simulated date and Start over.

## Sub-features

- `admin-students` is the Students page at `/admin` and the read-only Student detail at `/admin/students/<id>`. The page's `h1` is `Students`. Under it the overview counts read `<n> students` (`student` for 1), `<n> on a paid plan`, `<n> in a free week`, `<n> retrying or lapsed`, `<n> need analytics` (`needs` for 1), and `<n> have this week's schedule` (`has` for 1); they count every student and ignore search and filters. With no students the heading and the counts (all `0`) stay, and `No students have completed onboarding yet.` replaces the controls and the list. The `Search students` textbox (placeholder `Name or email`) matches part of the name or email, ignoring case and accents; `Clear search` empties it and keeps focus in the box. The `Filter` button (`aria-haspopup="menu"`) opens `role=menu[name="Filter"]`: under `Plan status`, `menuitemradio`s `All students`, `Paid`, `Free week`, `Retrying or lapsed`, and `No plan`, then `menuitemcheckbox`es `Needs analytics` and `Has this week's schedule`, each with its count. The menu stays open while you pick and closes on `Escape` (focus back on the button) or a tap outside. The button reads `Filter`, `Filter · <n>` for checked boxes, or the plan name with `+<n>`. Sort is `Name` (A to Z, by email when the name is empty), `Newest` (joined), or `Plan status` (Retrying, Lapsed, No plan, Free week, Paid). From 1024px the list is a table (`Student`, `Plan`, `Analytics` `Needed`/`Done`, `This week` `Ready`/`Held`/`None`, `Study time`, `Test`, `Joined`); the `Student`, `Plan`, and `Joined` headers are the sort buttons, with `aria-sort` on the sorted column, and `<n> students` (`student` for 1) shows beside the controls. Below 1024px the list is rows (name, badges, `<email> · <time> per week`) and sort is the `Sort: <name>` menu (`role=menu[name="Sort"]`), which closes on a pick. While search or a filter narrows the list, `Clear` (640px and up) or `Clear filters` beside `<n> students of <total>` (below 640px) resets them and keeps the sort. With nothing left, the list says `No students match.` with a `Clear filters` button. Every change rewrites the page link with `history.replaceState` (`q`, `plan=paid|free_week|trouble|no_plan`, `analytics=needed`, `week=ready`, `sort=newest|plan`; defaults are left out), so a reload keeps the search, filters, and sort. Each student link carries the same query, and `← All students` on Student detail returns to `/admin` with it. Student detail shows the facts card (name, email, badges, `Read only`, `Open in lab`) and the `role=tablist[name="Student history"]` tabs `This week`, `Past weeks`, `Progress`, and `Settings & tests` (`Settings` below 640px). A tab rewrites `?tab=` with `history.replaceState`, the arrow keys, `Home`, and `End` move between tabs, and the panel fades in. A tab shrinks to 97% while pressed (`scale` reads `0.97`) and not under reduced motion.
- `admin-frame` is the admin top bar: the Students and Schedule lab tabs (a bottom tab bar below 768px), the Admin / Student switch, and the avatar menu.
- `admin-lab` is the Schedule lab at `/admin/lab`: one `role=region[name="Inputs"]` card (`Preset` combobox, `Current PT score` and `Goal score` textboxes, `Goal test month`, `Test time`, the analytics radios, `First week`, the day capacity table with the practice-test day) beside `role=region[name="Built week"]`, which holds `role=region[name="Schedule reasons"]` (`Planned from <source>`) and the read-only day cards. It opens on Steady. Every change rewrites `?lab=` with `history.replaceState`; a reload reopens the same inputs, and an unreadable `?lab=` opens on Steady. An out-of-range value shows the live field error and `role=status` `Showing the last week that built. Fix the <fields>.` (only `Fix the <fields>.` when no week has built yet) `Open in lab` on a student's facts card opens `/admin/lab?student=<id>`: `Copied from <name>, <Mon D of their latest upload>. Edits here do not change the student.` with `Their upload` checked, or `<name> has no analytics yet, so the lab started from the Steady preset.` A student needs an approved snapshot for `Their upload`; a seeded student has none, so confirm one weekly upload as that student first (fixture setup). `Next week →` builds the following week (`Week <n> · <Mon D>`, `Planned from <direction> sample, week <n> upload`) and `← Earlier week` moves back; `?lab=` keeps the built count and the shown week, so a reload reopens the same week with later weeks kept. The last week says `Last week in the chain. <reason>` and turns Next week off: at 12 weeks, or before the week that starts on or after the goal test month. An input error keeps the chain and both buttons still move through it. The week element `.lab-week` animates `lab-week-next` or `lab-week-earlier` (`lab-week-fade` under reduced motion). Prove nothing is saved by comparing `inspect` before and after. The `Screenshots` analytics radio opens `role=list[name="Screenshots"]` with three `listitem`s (`Logical Reasoning`, `Reading Comprehension`, `Practice test sections`), each `Not added`, `Reading <file>…` (a pulsing dot), what it read (`<file> · <n> types`, `PT 158 · <n> missed`), or the live error below the list. Choose files through each slot's `input[type=file][data-slot="<Logical Reasoning|Reading Comprehension|practice test section>"]`. Until LR and RC are both read, `role=status` says `Showing the last week that built. Add the Logical Reasoning and Reading Comprehension screenshots.`; once both are read the week rebuilds with `Planned from Screenshots: <n> LR types · <n> RC types` (plus `· PT 158, <n> missed` with the four pt158 fixtures). Reads go to the extract routes with `?for=lab`, which skip the plan check, add to `scan_spending`, and refuse once the month total would pass 80% of `monthly_limit_usd`; then the slot shows the scanning-off message and the live `Scanning is off right now.` note shows, with sample directions and typed inputs still building. `?lab=` keeps `Screenshots` but never what was read.
- `admin-walkthrough` is the Walkthrough card on the Admin's own student side: the date in the switch, named jumps, Pick a day, and Start over.
- `admin-plan` shows each student's plan badge (`No plan`, `Free week`, `Paid`, `Retrying`, `Lapsed`; `Admin` for Admins) on the list and the read-only view, and a `Needs analytics` badge until the student has an approved snapshot. A held week reads `This student's weekly schedule for this week is held. It appears when their first payment goes through.` instead of the schedule. On the Students list a held week reads `Held` in the table's This week column and as a `Held` badge on the phone and tablet rows; it is not counted in `has this week's schedule` and does not match that filter.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- An Admin signs in and lands on `/admin`, the Students page. The list includes onboarded ordinary students and excludes Admin-owned profiles.
- Tap Students or Schedule lab in the top bar (Students or Lab in the phone tab bar). Tap the switch's `Student side` link (it reads `Student` below 1280px) to reach the Admin's own This week; tap `Admin` to return to the admin page used last.
- Choose a student's row on the Students page to open `/admin/students/<id>`. Use `← All students` to return.
- On the Admin's student side, the switch's Student segment is the date, a button named `Student side, real date <Ddd, Mmm D>. Walkthrough` (`simulated date` once one is set) with `aria-current="true"`. It reads `<Mmm D>` below 1024px and `<Ddd>, <Mmm D>` from 1024px, with a grey dot (amber when simulated) and a ⌄. Tapping it opens `role=region[name="Walkthrough"]` under the switch (full width under the bar below 768px) and moves focus to the card.

## Driving it with browser tools

Preconditions:

- Doctor passes. Seed an onboarded Admin with `local.mjs seed "$VERIFY_RUN_DIR" admin verify-admin@example.test`. Sign in by setting the cookie from `local.mjs session "$VERIFY_RUN_DIR" verify-admin@example.test`. For Students page and Student detail checks, also seed a separate student with the `student` role. The Admin's own profile cannot serve as that fixture.

- After Admin login, require `/admin`. Click the student's row by its displayed email (the table link from 1024px, the row link below). Require the `Read only` badge, the correct email, and the saved schedule or the explicit no-saved-schedule message. On a real Saturday or Sunday, a student with no current weekly schedule and a saved next-week preview shows `This student has saved their plan for next week. It starts Monday, <date>.` instead of the analytics message. A student who confirmed next week's upload but has no saved preview yet shows `This student has confirmed their upload for next week, which starts Monday, <date>. Their plan is saved when they open This week.` The Confirm redirect saves the preview at once, so reaching that state needs the preview row deleted as labelled fixture setup. This view uses the server's real date, so a weekend check needs the server clock on a weekend. Record weekly-schedule counts before and after opening. This view must not create a missing schedule. Require no check-off controls (`role=checkbox`). Test both the list link and the direct student URL. Search for part of the student's email, pick a filter, and change the sort; reload and require the same list and link. Open the student and require `← All students` to return to `/admin` with the same query.
- Press shrink on a Student detail tab: `page.mouse.down()` over the tab, wait about 300 ms, read `getComputedStyle(tab).scale` (`0.97`), then `page.mouse.up()`. In a `reducedMotion: "reduce"` context the held tab reads `none` or `1`.
- The top bar and the phone tab bar both hold the tabs and the switch, and one of each is hidden by width, so filter locators to visible ones (`.filter({ visible: true })`). Click `role=link[name="Student side"]` in `role=group[name="Side"]`. Require `/dashboard` and the Student segment marked `aria-current="true"`. Click `role=link[name="Admin"]` and require the admin page used last, with its link. A standard student opening any `/admin` link, including `/admin/lab` and an unknown `/admin/...` path, must land on their own This week (which sends a student with no schedule on to `/dashboard/upload`).
- Walkthrough card: the title row reads `Real date` and the date (`Monday, November 16`), or `Simulated · real date <Ddd, Mmm D>` in amber (`color` is the `--color-warning-ink` value). Under `Move the date` are buttons named `Next day: <Ddd, Mmm D>`, `Saturday: Weekly upload opens · <Mmm D>`, and `Monday: New week starts · <Mmm D>`. When tomorrow is Saturday or Monday, the Next day row names it (`Next day: Sat, Nov 21 · Weekly upload opens`) and that day's own row is gone. A tap calls the set-simulated-date action: the jumps, the calendar days, and Start over turn off and `role=status` reads `Moving…`, then the real page refreshes on the new date, the switch and the card title show it, and the status reads `Moved to <Ddd, Mmm D>.` Require `accounts.simulated_date` to match and no other record to change: a move saves no snapshot, schedule, or check-in. When the new date's week has no weekly schedule, This week opens the weekly upload at `/dashboard/upload` (`Plan this week`, or `Plan next week` on Saturday and Sunday), as for a real student who missed it. A refused move shows `Something went wrong. Try again.` in the status. `Pick a day` (`aria-expanded`) opens a Monday-first month, `role=group[name="<Month YYYY>"]`: earlier days and today are disabled (`<Ddd, Mmm D>, not available`, `<Ddd, Mmm D>, today`), later days up to 56 days ahead are `Move to <Ddd, Mmm D>`, and a day whose week has a saved weekly schedule adds `, week has a schedule` and a dot. `Earlier month` is off on the current month and `Later month` on the month of the 56th day. The server also refuses a date more than 56 days past the student side's date. The card closes at once on `Close Walkthrough`, `Escape`, a tap anywhere outside it (the real page included), or the date again, and focus returns to the date.
- Start over: click `role=button[name="Start over"]`. `role=group[name="Start over: confirm"]` lists `Deletes all weekly schedules, activity check-ins, analytics snapshots, practice-test results and study settings on your student side.` and `Keeps your admin account.`, with focus on `Cancel`. `Cancel` folds it with no record change and returns focus to `Start over`. `Confirm start over` reads `Starting over…` while it runs, then lands on `/onboarding` on the real date. Require no Student or test history, a null `simulated_date`, and the same Admin Account and role. Never use it on someone else's workspace.
- To give the Admin's student side a weekly schedule, run a weekly upload through the fake extractor (see [This week](weekly-schedule.md)). To reach a later week, move with the Walkthrough and upload again when This week opens the weekly upload.

## Gotchas

- A seeded Admin is setup, not proof of authorization. The `make-admin` command still needs a fresh sign-in after promotion; a seeded Admin signs in with the Admin role from the start.
- Start over uses one Postgres transaction.
- Each weekly upload confirm uses one of the Account's 5 analytics saves for the real day. Start over and the Simulated date do not give saves back. A recipe with two weekly uploads leaves 3; seed a second Admin for more.
- The top bar and the phone bar each hold a switch, and one is hidden by width, so take the visible date button (`.filter({ visible: true })`). Only the open card is in the page, so each card control name matches once. Use snapshot names rather than coordinates.
- The date and the Walkthrough show only on the Admin's student side (`/dashboard`, `/dashboard/upload`, `/progress`, `/settings`, `/onboarding`, `/account`), not on `/admin` or `/admin/students/<id>`. After an Admin visit to `/`, which lands on `/admin`, open `/dashboard` before the next control.
- Direct Admin views of students use the real date. Simulated date affects only the Admin's own student side.
- Account deletion is unavailable for Admins. Use the disposable student fixture for that separate path. After Start over, the Admin's `/account` redirects to `/onboarding` until they onboard again.
