# Sign-up and Account

DrillMap signs people in with Google or with an email-verified password. Signup offers "Continue with Google", whose press counts as agreement to the Terms and Privacy Policy, then a name, email, and password form with the Terms box above "Create account". Every password signup shows "Check your email". The email's link opens "Verify my email"; only the button press makes the Account. Login offers Google, then the email and password form, and explains why a sign-in failed or a session ended. Account shows the email and its sign-in method. A password Account can change its password and deletes its own records with its current password. A Google Account deletes them after a Google sign-in from the last 10 minutes.

## Sub-features

- `signup-entry` reaches Signup from the public navigation and landing action.
- `signup-consent` saves and sends nothing until the Terms and Privacy box is ticked, and shows "Tick the box to continue." beside the box, keeping the typed values.
- `password-signup` shows "Check your email" with the email, and the fake sender writes the verification email to `app.log`.
- `verify-email` opens `Verify your email` from the logged link without using it; `Verify my email` makes the Account and lands on `/login?notice=EmailVerified` with "Email verified. Log in." A used, expired, or unknown link shows "This link doesn't work".
- `password-login` signs the verified student in with the password and lands on onboarding.
- `login-states` shows "Continue with Google", then the email and password form with "Log in", and one message per reason: refused sign-in, Disabled Account, ended session ("Your session ended. Log in again."), wrong email or password, and email verified.
- `forgot-password` opens from "Forgot password?" on Login. Every email gets "Check your email" with "If <email> has a DrillMap Account, we sent it an email with your next step." A password Account gets the reset email in `app.log`.
- `reset-password` opens "Set a new password" from the logged link without using it. A refused password keeps the link. Saving lands on `/login?notice=PasswordChanged` with "Password changed. Log in with your new password." and ends the Account's sessions in every browser. A used, expired, or replaced link shows "This link doesn't work" with "Get a new link".
- `password-login-refusals` keeps the typed email and clears the password after each refusal. A wrong password and an unknown email show the same message between `Or with email` and the `Email` field, and mark no field. Each refusal replaces any notice from the address. A password over 72 bytes marks the Password field with "That password is too long. Try a shorter one." The 11th try for one email in 15 minutes shows "Too many tries. Login is paused for a while. Try again later, or reset your password." A pending signup's right password shows "Verify your email first. We sent you a new link. Check your spam folder if you don't see it." and logs a new link. A Disabled Account's right password lands on `/login?error=AccountDisabled`.
- `logout` returns to the public app with protected pages unavailable.
- `account-delete` removes the student's Account and associated records, adds one `deletion_list` row with the Account ID, and ends the Account's sessions in every browser.
- `account-delete-will-finish`: when step 2 fails after the mark, the student lands on `/login?notice=DeletionWillFinish` with "Your Account is being deleted." Every other browser of the Account is refused at once. A later sign-in with the right password finishes the deletion and lands on `/login?notice=AccountDeleted` with "Your Account is deleted."
- `account-change-password` shows only for a password Account (badge `Password sign-in`). A wrong current password shows "That's not your current password. Try again." and clears that field. A refused new password shows its message in place of the hint. A good change lands on `/login?notice=PasswordChanged` and ends the Account's sessions in every browser. A wrong try counts against the Login limits of the email.
- `account-delete-password` asks a password Account for its current password and `DELETE`, with no sign-in age. Wrong text shows the text message and counts no try. A wrong password clears the password field and keeps the Account.
- `account-delete-sign-in-age` asks for a new Google sign-in when the sign-in is more than 10 minutes old. "Continue with Google" there forces the Google account chooser and returns to `/account#delete-account`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- On `/`, click `Build my first plan` or the navigation `Sign up` link. Both reach `/signup`.
- On `/login`, click `Sign up`. On `/signup`, click `Log in`. Public navigation also has `Log in`.
- Auth.js sends every Google sign-in failure to `/login?error=<type>`. A refused Google sign-in (`AccessDenied`) and any other Auth.js error type show the refusal text. `AccountDisabled` and `SessionEnded` show their own text. A Google sign-in with a password Account's email goes to `EmailUsesPassword`; Postgres tests cover that refusal, because this baseline has no Google client. The proxy adds `SessionEnded` when the browser sent a session cookie that the current-Account check ended, on a protected page or on a direct `/login` visit.
- On `/login`, click `Forgot password?` beside the Password label. It reaches `/forgot-password`; its `Log in` link returns.
- In the student workspace, open `Account menu for verify-student@example.test`. Choose `Account` or `Log out`.
- Direct `/signup`, `/login`, and `/account` links must enforce the same sign-in rules. `/forgot-password` and `/reset-password` stay open signed in or out.

## Driving it with browser tools

Preconditions:

- Doctor passes. Start signed out with no Account for the chosen email.
- Save a database inspection before Signup. Start the browser action recording.

Steps:

- Open each Signup entry with `click('role=link[name="Build my first plan"]')` and `click('header a[href="/signup"]')` on fresh public visits. Expect the `Sign up for DrillMap` heading and `/signup`. The page has no site header or site footer; its own `DrillMap` wordmark and `Terms`, `Privacy`, and `AI disclosure` links replace them.
- On `/signup`, fill `Name`, `Email`, and `Password`, leave the box unticked, and click `role=button[name="Create account"]`. Expect `Tick the box to continue.` under the box, the box with `aria-invalid="true"`, the typed values kept, the URL still `/signup`, and no new Account record. Capture this state.
- Tick the box and click `Create account`. Expect the `Check your email` heading with the email, and no Account record. Read the newest `Fake email to <email>` entry in `app.log` and open its `/verify-email?token=` link. Expect `Verify your email` and no record change after a reload. Click `Verify my email`. Expect `/login?notice=EmailVerified`, the status `Email verified. Log in.`, and one Account with no Google ID. Open the link again and press the button: expect `This link doesn't work`. Before the link, log in with the email and password: expect the verify-your-email message and a newer link in `app.log`. After verification, log in with a wrong password, then an unknown email, then a password over 72 bytes, then the unknown email 9 more times: expect the refusal messages above, and `failedLoginForEmail` at 10 for the unknown email. On `/login`, type the email and password and click `Log in`. Expect `/onboarding` and `Set your target`. `smoke.mjs` drives these steps.
- For a reset, sign up and verify a disposable student through the UI, then log it in with the password in a second browser (an isolated Playwright context). In the first browser, open `/login`, click `Forgot password?`, fill `Email`, and click `Send reset link`. Expect `Check your email` and "A reset link works for 30 minutes." Read the newest `/reset-password?token=` link for the email in `app.log` and open it. Expect `Set a new password`, a `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer` response header, and no resource from another origin. Type `password123` in `New password` and click `Save new password`: expect the too-common message with the typed value kept. Type a good password and save: expect `/login?notice=PasswordChanged`. Reload the second browser: expect `/login?error=SessionEnded`. The old password is refused and the new one signs in. Open the used link again: expect `This link doesn't work`. Postgres tests cover the Google Account and pending-signup emails, the 30-minute expiry, replaced links, and the reset limits.
- Open `/login`. Expect the `Log in to DrillMap` heading, the buttons `Continue with Google` and `Log in`, and the `Email` and `Password` inputs. Open `/login?error=AccessDenied`, `/login?error=AccountDisabled`, `/login?error=SessionEnded`, and `/login?error=EmailUsesPassword`. Expect, in order, the alert `Google sign-in didn't finish. Try again, or use a Google account with a verified email.`, the alert `This Account can't log in.`, the status `Your session ended. Log in again.`, and the alert `This email uses a password. Log in with your password.` The first three sit above `Continue with Google`; the last sits between `Or with email` and the `Email` field. Capture each.
- Seed the student with `local.mjs seed "$VERIFY_RUN_DIR" student verify-student@example.test --not-onboarded`. Set its minted session cookie. Open `/onboarding` and expect `Set your target`. Complete [onboarding](study-settings.md) to obtain student navigation. Open `role=button[name="Account menu for verify-student@example.test"]`, then `role=menuitem[name="Account"]`. Expect `/account`, the `Account` heading, and the same email.
- Open the Account menu and click `role=menuitem[name="Log out"]`. Wait for a full page load of `/?signedOut=1` and `role=link[name="Build my first plan"]` on the landing page; a direct visit before that still lands signed in. Visit `/account` directly. Expect plain `/login` with no message: the browser sent no session cookie.
- For an old sign-in, seed a second student and mint its session with `local.mjs session "$VERIFY_RUN_DIR" <email> --signed-in-minutes-ago 15`. Open `/account`. Expect `Sign in again to delete`, `Deleting your account needs a Google sign-in from the last 10 minutes.`, and one `Continue with Google` button in the deletion card with no confirmation field. Click it. The browser reaches `accounts.google.com`, which refuses the empty baseline client ID. To read the address, POST the page's `$ACTION_ID_…` form field with that cookie and an `Origin: http://127.0.0.1:3100` header: require `prompt=select_account` in the `Location` header and `/account#delete-account` in the `authjs.callback-url` cookie.
- For deletion, use only this disposable student. Mint a second session in a second browser (an isolated Playwright context) and open `/account` there. Save records and affected IDs first. In the first browser, reach Account through its menu and through `/account`. Type `delete` into `role=textbox[name="Type DELETE to confirm"]` and click `role=button[name="Delete account"]`. Expect `Type DELETE in capital letters to confirm.` under the field, the field with `aria-invalid="true"` and still holding `delete`, and no record change. Type `DELETE` and click again. Expect `Deleting…` on the disabled button with a read-only field, then the public landing page. Inspect records and require the user's ID, their Student, analytics snapshots, practice-test results, weekly schedules, and activity check-ins to be absent. Reload `/account` in the second browser. Expect `/login?error=SessionEnded` and `Your session ended. Log in again.`
- For a password Account, sign up, verify, and onboard a disposable student through the UI, then log it in with the password in a second browser (an isolated Playwright context). In the first browser, open Account through its menu. Expect `Password sign-in`, the `Change password` card, and a `Current password` field in the deletion card with no `Sign in again to delete`. In the `Change password` card, type a wrong current password and a good new one and click `Change password`: expect `That's not your current password. Try again.`, an empty `Current password`, and the new password kept. Type the right current password and `short`: expect `That password is too short. Use at least 8 characters.` in place of the hint. Type a good new password: expect `/login?notice=PasswordChanged` with `Password changed. Log in with your new password.` and a `session_version` one higher. Reload `/account` in the second browser: expect `/login?error=SessionEnded` and `Your session ended. Log in again.` The old password is refused and the new one signs in. In the deletion card, type the right password and `delete`: expect `Type DELETE in capital letters to confirm.`, the password kept, and no change to `failedLoginForEmail`. Type a wrong password and `DELETE`: expect `That's not your current password. Try again.`, an empty password field, `DELETE` kept, and `failedLoginForEmail` one higher. Type the right password: expect the landing page and no Account or Student for the email. Seed a Google student and open its `/account`: expect `Google sign-in` and no `Change password` card or `Current password` field.

## Gotchas

- A signed-in student visiting `/signup` or `/login` redirects to `/dashboard`. From there, an Account without a Student goes to `/onboarding`, and a week without a weekly schedule goes to `/dashboard/upload`. An Admin lands on `/admin`.
- To force step 2 to fail, connect to the run's database as the owner and add a `before delete` trigger on `activity_check_ins` that raises. Drop it before the retry. `inspect` does not read `deleting_since` or `deletion_list`; read them with `psql` on the run database.
- Admin Accounts have no deletion form. Their deletion card shows `Admin account` and `This action is not available for admin accounts.` Verify student deletion with a student Account.
- A minted session counts as a fresh sign-in for 10 minutes. Mint the deleting student's session just before the deletion steps.
- This baseline supplies no Google client. Do not click `Continue with Google`, and do not use the user's connected Google account. Real Google sign-in, its refusals, and the Disabled Account refusal at sign-in are checked by the owner on the copy with a Google test user. Record them as untested here.
- UI validation alone proves no write only when the read-only database check also shows no new user.
